Privacy Policy

NutriFlow · Effective date: February 24, 2026

NutriFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal nutrition and health tracking application. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and username (required to create an account)
  • Password (stored securely by our authentication provider)

Health & Body Data

  • Height, weight, birth year, and gender
  • Goal weight and nutrition goals (calorie, protein, carb, and fat targets)
  • Diet type and dietary preferences
  • Fitness level indicators

Nutrition Tracking Data

  • Meals logged (name, meal time, ingredients, nutritional values)
  • Water intake records
  • Weight tracking entries

Photos

  • Meal images you choose to capture via camera or select from your photo library. Photos are stored locally on your device.

Location Data (opt-in)

  • If you choose to enable it, NutriFlow may collect your device's location when you log a meal. This is entirely optional and requires your explicit permission. Location data is used solely to associate meals with a place (e.g., "lunch at home" vs. "dinner at a restaurant") and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device settings or within the app.

Preferences & Goals

  • Main goals, eating habit goals, and personal motivators you select during onboarding or in settings.

Automatically Collected

  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or analytics/tracking data. Location data is only collected if you explicitly opt in (see "Location Data" above).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the NutriFlow experience (tracking meals, calculating nutritional summaries, and suggesting goals).
  • To authenticate your account and sync your data across sessions.
  • To compute nutrition plans based on your body measurements and goals.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a cloud database powered by Supabase, hosted on secure servers with row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring that only you can access your own data. Communication between the app and the server is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

Meal photos remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

NutriFlow uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase – authentication and database hosting (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts – font delivery; may collect IP addresses per Google's Privacy Policy

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or social media SDKs.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access – request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction – update inaccurate information via the app settings.
  • Deletion – request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Data Portability – request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

NutriFlow is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
