2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address – required to create and secure your account.
  • Username / display name – used to personalise your in-app experience.
  • Password – hashed and stored securely by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never store passwords in plain text.

Health & Body Data

NutriMax is a nutrition and health-tracking app. To provide its core features we collect the following health-related information that you enter voluntarily:

  • Height and current weight
  • Birth year (used to calculate age-appropriate nutritional recommendations)
  • Biological sex / gender
  • Goal weight and target macronutrient splits (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat)
  • Diet type (e.g. vegan, vegetarian, omnivore) and dietary preferences or restrictions
  • Fitness / activity level

This data falls within the category of health data under GDPR Article 9. We process it exclusively on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) to deliver the nutrition-tracking features you have requested.

Nutrition & Meal Tracking Data

  • Meals you log: name, meal time, food items, portion sizes, and nutritional values
  • Water intake records
  • Daily / weekly / monthly weight entries

Photos (Camera & Photo Library)

  • Meal images you choose to capture with the camera or select from your photo library. Photos are stored locally on your device. If you use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, the selected meal photo is transmitted to our third-party AI service provider for processing as described in Section 7.

AI Meal Estimation Inputs (optional)

  • If you choose to use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, NutriMax may send the meal photo you selected and any meal description or other text you enter for that feature to our third-party AI service provider to generate nutrition estimates.

Location Data (opt-in)

  • If you choose to enable it, NutriMax may record your approximate device location when you log a meal (e.g., to tag "lunch at home" vs. "dinner at a restaurant"). Location access is entirely optional, requires your explicit permission, and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • You can grant or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

Preferences & Goals

  • Your main wellness goals, eating-habit targets, and personal motivators entered during onboarding or updated in app settings.

Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • We do not collect persistent device identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, or behavioural analytics/tracking data.
  • Supabase may temporarily process your IP address as part of secure session management (see Section 7).

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases for each processing activity. The table below maps every data category to its specific legal basis:

Consent — Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR

Used for health-related special-category data and optional features that require your explicit permission. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 5).

  • Height, weight, birth year, biological sex – health data (Art. 9)
  • Goal weight, macronutrient targets, diet type – health data (Art. 9)
  • Fitness / activity level – health data (Art. 9)
  • Dietary preferences / restrictions – health data (Art. 9)
  • Location data – opt-in only; used for meal location tagging
  • Meal photos – opt-in only; stored locally on device unless you submit them for optional AI-based nutrition estimation
  • AI meal estimation inputs – optional meal photos and text you submit for AI-based nutrition estimation

Performance of a contract — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR

Used for data that is necessary to provide the NutriMax service you have requested.

  • Email address – required for account creation, login, and communication
  • Username / display name – required for in-app personalisation
  • Password (hashed) – required for account security
  • Meal logs (food items, portion sizes, nutritional values, meal times) – core service feature
  • Water intake records – core service feature
  • Daily / weekly weight entries – core service feature
  • Wellness goals & personal motivators – core service feature

Legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR

Used for processing that is necessary to protect the security and integrity of NutriMax. You have the right to object (see Section 11).

  • IP address – temporarily processed by Supabase during authentication sessions for fraud prevention and rate limiting; not stored long-term by us
  • Session tokens – used to maintain your authenticated session securely

Where we rely on consent, you have the right to withdraw it at any time. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you have the right to object (see Section 11).

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • Authentication & account management – to create, secure, and maintain your NutriMax account and to sync your data across your devices.
  • Nutrition tracking & personalisation – to log meals, calculate calorie and macronutrient summaries, and display progress towards your goals.
  • Health recommendations – to compute personalised daily calorie targets and macronutrient ranges based on your body measurements, activity level, and goals.
  • Meal photo storage – to let you attach images to meal entries. Photos are stored on-device unless you choose to submit them for optional AI-based nutrition estimation.
  • AI-based nutrition estimation – to analyze a meal photo and/or meal description that you choose to submit and return estimated nutrition data.
  • Location tagging (opt-in) – to associate meal entries with a location if you have granted location permission.
  • Goal & reminder features – to present your nutrition goals and, if enabled, to send local (on-device) reminders.

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioural profiling, or selling to third parties.

5. Consent & How to Withdraw It

When consent is collected:

  • During onboarding, when you enter your health and body data (height, weight, goals, diet type, etc.) for the first time.
  • When you enable optional features such as location tagging or meal photo capture and grant the corresponding device permission.
  • When you intentionally submit a meal photo and/or meal description for the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature.

How to withdraw consent:

  • Device permissions – revoke camera, photo library, or location access at any time via your device's Settings app. The app will continue to work; only the specific feature tied to that permission will be disabled.
  • AI-based nutrition estimation – stop using the optional AI estimation feature. If you do not submit a meal photo or meal description for estimation, that data will not be sent to the third-party AI service.
  • Health / body data – delete individual data points in the app's profile or settings screens, or delete your entire account to remove all data (see Section 10).
  • Full withdrawal – delete your NutriMax account (in-app or via email). This permanently removes all personal data from our servers and effectively withdraws all consent.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

6. Device Permissions

NutriMax may request the following permissions on your device:

Camera

Used only when you choose to photograph a meal. Images are saved locally on your device. If you use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, the selected image may also be transmitted to our third-party AI service provider for processing. You can deny camera permission without affecting any other feature of the app.

Photo Library / Storage

Used only when you choose to attach an existing photo to a meal entry. Access is limited to photos you explicitly select. Images remain on your device unless you submit a selected image for optional AI-based nutrition estimation.

Location (optional)

Used only when you opt in to meal location tagging (see Section 2). The app requests "while using" location access; it does not request background location.

Notifications (optional)

Used only to deliver local, on-device reminders (e.g., meal or water-intake reminders) that you configure within the app. We do not use push notification services that transmit your data to external servers.

Microphone

NutriMax does not request or use microphone access.

Device Identifiers

NutriMax does not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) or other persistent device identifiers for tracking purposes.

9. Data Storage & Security

  • Encryption in transit – all communication between the NutriMax app and our backend is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Encryption at rest – Supabase encrypts data at rest using AES-256 on managed cloud infrastructure.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) – database access policies ensure that each user can only read and write their own data; no cross-user data access is possible.
  • Access controls – only authorised GME Digital personnel have access to production infrastructure, and access is protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Meal photos – stored on your device. If you use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, the selected photo is also transmitted securely to OpenAI for processing.
  • No plain-text passwords – passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth before storage.

While we take industry-standard precautions to protect your data, no system is completely immune to security risks. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your device software up to date.

10. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your NutriMax account is active. Once you delete your account, all associated personal data (account info, health data, nutrition logs) will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers within 30 days.

How to delete your account

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.
  • By email: Send a deletion request to main.gme.digital@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will confirm deletion within 5 business days and complete it within 30 days.

For a step-by-step guide, visit our Account Deletion page.

Note: Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete your account or cloud data. Please use one of the methods above to request full data removal.

11. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access – request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification / Correction – update or correct inaccurate information via the app's profile and settings screens, or by contacting us.
  • Erasure / Deletion – request deletion of your account and all associated data (see Section 10).
  • Data Portability – request your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON or CSV).
  • Restriction of Processing – request that we limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Objection – object to certain types of data processing.
  • Withdraw Consent – where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting lawfulness of prior processing.

CCPA (California Residents): You have the right to know what personal information we collect and share, the right to delete your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at main@gmedigital.de. We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA). We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

If you are located in the EU/EEA and believe your data rights are being infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child below the applicable age threshold, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.
