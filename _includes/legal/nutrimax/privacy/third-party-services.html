NutriMax uses a small number of carefully selected third-party services. Each service operates under its own privacy policy and security controls:

Supabase (Authentication & Database)

Supabase powers our cloud authentication and database backend. Account credentials, health/body data, and nutrition logs are stored in Supabase-managed PostgreSQL databases protected by row-level security (RLS). Shared meals and their photos (see Shared Meals) are also stored with Supabase. Supabase processes data on infrastructure hosted in the EU (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt).
Supabase Privacy Policy →

OpenAI (AI-based Nutrition Estimation)

NutriMax uses OpenAI to provide an optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature. When you choose to use this feature, the meal photo you submit and any related text you enter are transmitted to OpenAI for processing to generate estimated nutrition information. We do not use OpenAI for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.
OpenAI Privacy Policy →

Open Food Facts (Product Barcode Lookup)

When you scan or type a product barcode, NutriMax looks up the product in Open Food Facts, an open database of food products. Barcodes are decoded on your device. The lookup is made by our server, not by your phone: only the barcode number and the app's language are sent to Open Food Facts, never your user ID, your account data, or your IP address. Our server caches the answers by barcode and language only. Product data © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

No advertising or analytics SDKs

NutriMax does not integrate any advertising networks, cross-app tracking SDKs, social-media SDKs, or third-party analytics services. We do not sell or share your data with data brokers or marketing companies.
