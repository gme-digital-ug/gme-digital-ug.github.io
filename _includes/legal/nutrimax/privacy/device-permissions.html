NutriMax may request the following permissions on your device:

Camera

Used only when you choose to photograph a meal or to scan a product barcode. Barcodes are decoded on your device; only the barcode number is used for the product lookup (see {% include legal/ref.html id="third-party-services" %}). Meal photos are saved locally on your device. If you use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, the selected image may also be transmitted to our third-party AI service provider for processing. If you share a meal with another NutriMax user, its photo is stored with the shared copy (see Shared Meals). You can deny camera permission without affecting any other feature of the app.

Photo Library / Storage

Used only when you choose to attach an existing photo to a meal entry. Access is limited to photos you explicitly select. Images remain on your device unless you submit a selected image for optional AI-based nutrition estimation or share the meal with another NutriMax user (see Shared Meals).

Location (optional)

Used only when you opt in to meal location tagging (see {% include legal/ref.html id="information-we-collect" plain=true %}). The app requests "while using" location access; it does not request background location.

Notifications (optional)

Used only to deliver local, on-device reminders (e.g., meal or water-intake reminders) that you configure within the app. We do not use push notification services that transmit your data to external servers.

Microphone

NutriMax does not request or use microphone access.

Device Identifiers

NutriMax does not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) or other persistent device identifiers for tracking purposes.
