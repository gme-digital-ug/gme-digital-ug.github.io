Account Information

  • Email address – required to create and secure your account.
  • Username / display name – used to personalise your in-app experience.
  • Password – hashed and stored securely by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never store passwords in plain text.

Health & Body Data

NutriMax is a nutrition and health-tracking app. To provide its core features we collect the following health-related information that you enter voluntarily:

  • Height and current weight
  • Birth year (used to calculate age-appropriate nutritional recommendations)
  • Biological sex / gender
  • Goal weight and target macronutrient splits (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat)
  • Diet type (e.g. vegan, vegetarian, omnivore) and dietary preferences or restrictions
  • Fitness / activity level

This data falls within the category of health data under GDPR Article 9. We process it exclusively on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) to deliver the nutrition-tracking features you have requested.

Nutrition & Meal Tracking Data

  • Meals you log: name, meal time, food items, portion sizes, and nutritional values
  • Water intake records
  • Daily / weekly / monthly weight entries

Photos (Camera & Photo Library)

  • Meal images you choose to capture with the camera or select from your photo library. Photos are stored locally on your device. If you use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, the selected meal photo is transmitted to our third-party AI service provider for processing as described in {% include legal/ref.html id="third-party-services" %}. If you share a meal with another NutriMax user, its photo is stored on our servers together with the shared copy and deleted with it (see Shared Meals).

AI Meal Estimation Inputs (optional)

  • If you choose to use the optional AI-based nutrition estimation feature, NutriMax may send the meal photo you selected and any meal description or other text you enter for that feature to our third-party AI service provider to generate nutrition estimates.

Shared Meals (optional)

You can share a meal with another NutriMax user by link. When you choose Share with a NutriMax user, the app stores a copy of that meal on our servers (Supabase, in the EU: AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt) together with your user ID. The copy contains:

  • the meal's name, description, diet label, and portion size
  • its nutritional values
  • its ingredients, including the barcode of any product you scanned
  • the meal photo, if the meal has one

The copy does not include when you ate the meal or your name. Sharing a meal is the only feature that stores a meal photo on our servers. We use your user ID only to limit how many share links you can create (20 per day) and to delete your shared meals when you delete your account.

For 7 days, anyone who has the link and a NutriMax account can view the shared meal and import a copy of it; guest users cannot open it. We do not record who imports a shared meal, and you are not notified when someone does. After 7 days the link stops working, and the shared copy and its photo are deleted from our servers within a day. If you delete your account, your shared meals and their photos are deleted immediately.

Shared meals are used only to provide this feature. They are not used for any other purpose and are not passed on to third parties. We process them to provide the sharing feature you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Because a shared meal may reveal information about your health or diet, we also rely on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by choosing to share a meal after a one-time notice in the app.

Location Data (opt-in)

  • If you choose to enable it, NutriMax may record your approximate device location when you log a meal (e.g., to tag "lunch at home" vs. "dinner at a restaurant"). Location access is entirely optional, requires your explicit permission, and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • You can grant or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

Preferences & Goals

  • Your main wellness goals, eating-habit targets, and personal motivators entered during onboarding or updated in app settings.

Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • We do not collect persistent device identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, or behavioural analytics/tracking data.
  • Supabase may temporarily process your IP address as part of secure session management (see {% include legal/ref.html id="third-party-services" %}).
