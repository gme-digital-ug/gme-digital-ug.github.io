We retain your personal data for as long as your NutriMax account is active. Once you delete your account, all associated personal data (account info, health data, nutrition logs) will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers.

Shared meals are kept for a shorter time: a share link works for 7 days, after which the shared copy and its photo are deleted from our servers within a day. If you delete your account, your shared meals and their photos are deleted immediately (see Shared Meals).

How to delete your account

You can delete your account in the app or by sending us an email. Our Delete account page shows both ways step by step and says how long each one takes.

Note: Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete your account or cloud data. Please delete your account in the app or by email to request full data removal.
