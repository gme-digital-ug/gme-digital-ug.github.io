Minesweeper is a single-player logic puzzle game. It lets you:

  • Play the classic "clear the board without hitting a mine" game;
  • Choose from difficulty presets or create custom boards;
  • Place flags, restart games, and switch between light and dark themes.

The Service runs entirely on your device and does not require an account or an internet connection to play.
