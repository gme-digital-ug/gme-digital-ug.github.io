Delete Your Account

You can delete your NutriMax account directly in the app or by sending us an email request.

Option 1 — In-App

Delete your account from within the app

Follow these four steps to permanently delete your account and all associated data directly inside NutriMax. The deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

1
Open the sidebar
2
Navigate to Settings
3
Scroll down & tap "Delete Account"
4
Confirm the deletion
Option 2 — By Email

Request account deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request the deletion of your account by sending an email to us.

📧 Send a deletion request

Write an email to main.gme.digital@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the following information:

  • Email address linked to your NutriMax account
✉️ Send deletion request
⏱ Deletion requests submitted by email are processed within 2 business days (Germany). You will receive a confirmation once your account and all associated data have been permanently removed.
