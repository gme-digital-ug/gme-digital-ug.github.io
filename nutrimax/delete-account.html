Delete Your Account

You can delete your NutriMax account directly in the app or by sending us an email request.

Option 1 — In-App

Delete your account from within the app

Follow these four steps to permanently delete your account and all associated data directly inside NutriMax: open the sidebar (☰), tap User Settings, scroll down to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account, then tick I understand this is permanent and tap Delete Account. The deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Deleting your account also immediately deletes any meals you shared by link, including their photos, and those links stop working.

1
Open the sidebar
2

3

4

Option 2 — By Email

Request account deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request the deletion of your account by sending an email to us.

📧 Send a deletion request

Write an email to {{ site.data.company.email }} with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the following information:

  • Email address linked to your NutriMax account
✉️ Send deletion request
⏱ We confirm deletion requests submitted by email within 5 business days (Germany) and complete the deletion within 30 days. We will let you know once your account and all associated data have been permanently removed.
