What is Minesweeper?

A fair, modern take on a timeless puzzle

Minesweeper is the logic game you already know — clear the board without detonating a single mine — rebuilt from the ground up with a clean interface, smooth controls, and rules that play fair.

Your first reveal is always safe, and when the board genuinely forces a 50/50 guess, you won't be punished for it. Tune the challenge with four difficulty presets or design your own board, from a cosy 5×5 to a sprawling 30×30 minefield.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Just you, the grid, and the satisfying click of a perfectly cleared board — on your phone, tablet, or desktop.

Features

Everything you love, nothing in the way

Faithful to the original where it counts, and refined everywhere else.

🛡️

Always-Safe First Click

Your opening reveal can never be a mine, so every game starts with a real opportunity to play.

🧠

Fair-Guess Protection

When the board forces an unavoidable 50/50, a mine won't be hiding under your guess. Gamble when a safe move exists, though, and you're on your own.

🎚️

Presets & Custom Boards

Easy, Normal, Hard, and Extreme presets — or craft your own grid from 5×5 up to 30×30 with the mine count you choose.

🚩

Flexible Flagging

Long-press to flag, or switch on flag mode for tap-to-flag. Mark suspected mines the way that suits you.

🎨

Light & Dark Themes

Match your system or pick Light or Dark by hand. A responsive layout adapts cleanly from phone to desktop.

🔒

Private by Design

No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no data collection. The game runs entirely on your device — fully offline.
